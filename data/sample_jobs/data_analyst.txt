Data Analyst

Responsibilities
- Analyze business data and build dashboards for stakeholders.
- Write SQL queries and clean datasets with Python.
- Present findings and recommendations to non-technical teams.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Computer Science, or Mathematics.
- 1+ years of experience in data analysis.
- Proficiency in SQL and Python (Pandas).
- Experience with Tableau or Power BI.
- Strong communication skills.

Nice to have
- Knowledge of machine learning.
