AI Engineer

About the role
We are building an AI-powered recruitment platform and are looking for an AI Engineer to own model development and serving.

Responsibilities
- Build and evaluate machine learning and deep learning models for text and image data.
- Develop REST APIs that serve models to web and mobile applications.
- Deploy and monitor models in production using Docker.
- Work with PostgreSQL and vector databases to support semantic search.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Informatics, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 2+ years of experience building machine learning applications.
- Strong Python skills and hands-on experience with TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Experience with FastAPI, Docker, and PostgreSQL.
- Familiarity with NLP.

Nice to have
- Experience with LLM applications and RAG.
- Kubernetes or AWS experience.
